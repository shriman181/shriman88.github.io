The Problem

Mid-market M&A runs on
manual grind.

Every mid-market deal buries associates in thousands of unstructured documents. Red flags hide in footnotes. Deals stall. Mistakes reach closing.

Before Mirex

  • ✗ Associates spend 60–80% of diligence time simply reading and categorizing documents with no leverage from prior deals
  • ✗ Red flags buried in footnotes, side letters, and attachments regularly survive manual review undetected

Financial Analysis

Parse three-statement models, management accounts, and audit reports. Surface anomalies, revenue trends, and working capital adjustments with source citations.

→Revenue recognition policy analysis and consistency check across periods
→Unusual transaction detection: related-party flows, one-time items, timing shifts
→Working capital normalization recommendations with historical variance analysis
→Debt and off-balance-sheet obligation mapping (leases, guarantees, earn-outs)

Legal & Contracts

Read every commercial agreement, lease, and side letter. Extract change-of-control provisions, assignment restrictions, and material breach definitions.

→Change-of-control clause extraction across all counterparty agreements
→Assignment restriction and consent requirement mapping for deal structuring
→Indemnity cap, basket, and survival period comparison against playbook
→Auto-draft SPA Schedule 3 (Disclosure Letter) from extracted data room facts

IP & Technology

Verify IP ownership chains, open-source license compliance, and contractor assignment completeness. Essential for any tech-enabled target.

→Contractor IP assignment gap analysis — identify unassigned cohorts by year
→Open-source license compliance review across disclosed codebases
→Patent and trademark registration status verification
→Key-person dependency mapping for core technology and systems

Compliance & Regulatory

Surface licensing gaps, pending regulatory actions, data privacy exposure, and environmental liability — before they reach the indemnity schedule.

→License and permit coverage map vs. operating jurisdictions
→Data privacy gap analysis (GDPR, DPDP Act, CCPA) from privacy policy and contracts
→Government notice and regulatory correspondence flagging
→Anti-bribery and sanctions screening against disclosed counterparty lists

HR & Equity

Analyze cap tables, ESOP pools, key employee agreements, and employment liability — including change-of-control acceleration triggers.

→ESOP pool vs. board-authorized limit verification and dilution modeling
→Acceleration clause detection in option grants and RSU agreements
→Key employee retention risk scoring and non-compete enforceability review
→Benefits and statutory compliance gap analysis (PF, Gratuity, ESIC for India)

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Arpan Das founded Mirex after spending years watching mid-market M&A deals slow to a crawl under the weight of manual due diligence. Having worked closely with deal teams across buy-side advisory and legal practice, he saw the same pattern repeat: brilliant associates buried in thousands of documents, red flags surfacing late, and counsel drafting disclosure schedules from scratch on every transaction.

His conviction is straightforward — the cognitive work of due diligence has not fundamentally changed in decades, but the technology to automate it now exists. The question was never whether AI could read contracts faster than humans. The question was whether it could be trusted to do so with the precision deal teams require.

"The purpose of due diligence is judgment — knowing what matters and why. Mirex handles the reading so deal teams can focus on the thinking."

Mirex is built on the belief that the first AI-native M&A intelligence layer will be won by a team with deep domain expertise in deal process, not just ML engineering. Arpan has assembled a founding team that combines M&A legal and financial experience with applied AI research — the two sides of the product that cannot be separated.

Common questions

How is data security handled?
All data room documents are processed in isolated, deal-specific environments. No document from one deal is ever accessible in another. SOC 2 Type II certification in process.
What VDRs does Mirex integrate with?
Mirex accepts direct upload of any VDR export. Native API integrations with Datasite, Intralinks, and Ansarada are available on Platform and Enterprise tiers.
Does Mirex replace legal counsel?
No — Mirex handles document review and first-draft legal language. All outputs are reviewed and finalized by qualified counsel. Think of Mirex as the world's fastest paralegal team.
How accurate is the red flag extraction?
In internal testing against manually reviewed data rooms, Mirex achieves 94% recall of material red flags at 91% precision. Every finding includes a source citation to verify independently.
Can we customize the clause library?
Platform and Enterprise tiers support full clause library customization — upload your firm's standard agreements and Mirex uses them as the benchmark for drafting and deviation detection.
Is there a free trial?
We offer a pilot on a single live or redacted historical deal at no charge for qualified PE funds and advisory firms. Book a demo to discuss eligibility.
